# EDU-001 — Purpose of Education

**Type:** Concept  
**Status:** Accepted (provisional)  
**Scope:** Education — Global  
**Owner:** Community  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-21

## Summary

Education should prioritize the formation of human beings capable of living wisely, caring for themselves and others, cooperating, adapting, and contributing to maintaining or improving civilization. Technical skills are important instruments in service of that human development.

## Core principle

Human development is the guiding criterion for educational decisions. Curricula, methods, and assessments should be evaluated according to their contribution to:

- personal autonomy and self-care;
- care for others and community;
- cooperation and civic life;
- critical thinking and ethical judgment;
- adaptive capacity across changing contexts;
- capacity to sustain, rebuild, and improve civilization.
