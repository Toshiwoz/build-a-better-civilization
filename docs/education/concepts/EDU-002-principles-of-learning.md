# EDU-002 — Principles of Learning

**Type:** Concept  
**Status:** Draft  
**Scope:** Education — Global  
**Owner:** Community  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-21

## Summary

Effective learning is active, meaningful, and context-sensitive. This concept doc outlines the foundational principles that should inform how educational programs are designed and evaluated across all levels.

## Core principles

1. **Meaningful engagement:** Learning is deeper when it connects to real problems, curiosity, or lived experience.
2. **Autonomy and agency:** Learners progress faster and retain more when they have meaningful choice and ownership of their learning path.
3. **Feedback loops:** Timely, specific, and actionable feedback is essential to growth. Grades alone are insufficient.
4. **Competence over credentials:** Progress should reflect demonstrated capability, not time spent or age.
5. **Social and emotional foundation:** Psychological safety, belonging, and self-regulation are prerequisites for learning, not extras.
6. **Transfer and application:** The test of learning is the ability to apply knowledge in new contexts, not reproduce it in familiar ones.
